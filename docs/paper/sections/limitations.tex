Several limits bound the claims.
Only two seeds and one open-weight 8B instruct model are studied.
Additional seeds are required before SI gossip responses can be declared unstable in a distributional sense rather than merely flipped once.
Forced institutional assignment precludes causal SI-versus-SFI identification.
Shock and democracy rounds coincide, so climate-damage effects are confounded with rule politics.
Gossip targeting is reconstructed from eligibility rules rather than from a complete verbatim bulletin archive.
Agent identifiers are not stable personas across seeds.

The environment is a stylized mechanism laboratory.
It is not a calibrated model of Parties, pledges, or disbursement under the Fund for responding to Loss and Damage.
Ostromian design principles are not fully implemented.
Positive mean cooperation must not be read as proof of internalized norms.
Boundary free-choice baselines motivate the climate package but are not re-run under the same multi-seed protocol here.
External validity beyond this prompt family, model, and parameter whitelist remains open.
